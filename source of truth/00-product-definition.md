# Hospital Digital Operations Platform — Product Definition

**Baseline:** v1.0 | **Market:** Bangladesh first | **Delivery:** dedicated installation per hospital, shared codebase.

## Product vision
A configurable platform for hospital websites, approved operational information, appointments, patient enquiries, feedback and management visibility. It is not a HIMS, EMR or clinical decision-support system.

## Website experience baseline

The supplied visual-reference specification is the benchmark for the public website's visual quality, page hierarchy, responsive behavior, navigation patterns, accessibility and performance workflow. Conduct a route/component/design-system audit and measure the reference before implementation. Recreate the relevant hospital-product page types using original branding and authorized assets. This reference informs presentation and engineering quality; it does not expand the agreed MVP into a full clinical-content portal or HIMS.

## Target stakeholders
Hospital owner/director, administrator, department manager, front-desk staff, marketing staff, IT administrator, patients/visitors and agency support operators.

## Release 1 — Commercial MVP
- Responsive Bangla/English public website.
- Hospital profile, branches, hours, contacts and emergency contact details.
- Doctor directory, specialties, qualifications, departments, services, fees and schedules.
- Doctor search and filters.
- Staff dashboard with roles, permissions and branch scoping.
- Draft/review/approve/publish workflow and change history.
- Appointment availability, requests, staff confirmation, cancellation and rescheduling.
- Website AI assistant grounded in approved public information; Bangla/English with reasonable Banglish input.
- Contact/callback and private feedback forms.
- Basic operational analytics.
- Dedicated deployment, backups, monitoring, access controls and training.

## Release 2 — Paid add-ons
WhatsApp Business, Facebook Messenger, SMS reminders, cross-channel human handover, AI-assisted staff update drafting, complaint classification/routing, scheduled reports, advanced analytics and multi-branch workflows.

## Release 3 — Enterprise/custom
HIMS/CRM/diagnostic integrations, patient report access after separate security/compliance design, local/private AI inference where justified, SSO, audit exports and additional languages.

## Explicit MVP exclusions
No EMR, prescriptions, diagnosis, treatment plans, symptom triage, clinical advice, lab/radiology report storage, billing, insurance, pharmacy, inpatient/bed/OT management, automatic AI publishing or guaranteed third-party integrations without supported APIs.

## Packages
- **Essential:** website, information dashboard, doctor schedules, basic appointment requests, audit history and deployment.
- **Professional:** Essential + website AI, richer appointment workflow, feedback and analytics.
- **Enterprise:** Professional + multi-branch controls, advanced roles/approvals, channel integrations, custom reporting/integrations and contracted support.

Exact prices, quotas, hosting, support hours and message allowances belong in each proposal. Avoid unlimited AI/messaging promises.

## Commercial model
One-time discovery/setup/implementation fee + recurring hosting/maintenance/support + optional modules + metered or pass-through AI/SMS/WhatsApp charges + separately scoped custom integration work.

## Key success measures
Hospital signs off on content; staff can maintain routine information; appointments are traceable and not double-booked; AI abstains safely; installation is isolated and recoverable; hospital accepts paid renewal/reference.

## Decisions to confirm with first customer
Branches, doctor count, appointment rules, fee presentation, approval matrix, messaging providers and consent, hosting region, retention, support SLA, legal review, patient login requirement (default: no login), and whether scheduled reports are MVP or Release 2.
